Account & data deletion

Delete your Interview Trainer account and data.

Follow the steps below to delete your account in the Android app, or request deletion without reinstalling the app.

Show deletion steps  ↓I no longer have the app

In the app: account data is deleted immediately when deletion succeeds. By email: requests are completed within 90 days of receipt. Backup copies and Firebase data are retained for up to 90 days, as detailed below.

In the Android app

How to delete your account

Sign in to the Interview Trainer account you want to delete.

Profile→Account Settings→Danger Zone→Delete Account

  1. Open your profile

    On a main app tab, tap the circle with your initials in the top-right corner to open Profile & Settings.

  2. Open Account Settings

    Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of your profile screen.

  3. Find Danger Zone

    Scroll down in Account Settings. Under Danger Zone, tap the red Delete Account row.

  4. Verify your identity and delete

    If your account has an app password: enter your current Interview Trainer password, then tap Delete Account.

    If your account has no app password: tap the Google-style Delete Account button and verify the Google account linked to Interview Trainer.

    After a successful deletion, the app signs you out and returns to the sign-in screen. If an error appears, deletion has not been confirmed; follow the error message and try again.

Deletion is permanent. Your account and training history cannot be restored through the app. Signing out or uninstalling the app is not an account deletion request.

Screenshots use demonstration profile details. Labels may be translated according to your device language.

Without the app

Request account deletion by email

You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled Interview Trainer or can no longer sign in. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Email support: support@interviewtrainer.app

Request account deletion by email

  1. Send a deletion request

    Email the support address above with the subject Interview Trainer — Account deletion request. Include the email address associated with your Interview Trainer account.

  2. Confirm ownership

    Support may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deleting it. Do not send your password, verification codes, Google ID tokens or payment card details by email.

  3. Receive confirmation

    We complete account and associated-data deletion within 90 days of receiving your email request and confirm the outcome by email.

What happens to your information

Data deletion and retention

Deleting your account removes the profile and training data listed below. Deletion through the app takes effect immediately after successful confirmation. Requests sent by email are completed within 90 days of receipt. Backup copies and Firebase data follow the separate retention periods shown below.

Other things to know

What about my PRO subscription?

You can manage or cancel a Google Play subscription separately in Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. A deletion request is not a refund request. See Google Play’s subscription instructions.

Will this delete my Google account?

No. This request concerns your Interview Trainer account. Your Google account is managed separately by Google.

I cannot sign in. Can I still request deletion?

Yes. Email support@interviewtrainer.app and include the email address linked to your Interview Trainer account. You can initiate a deletion request without installing the app. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account.

Does signing out delete my account?

No. Signing out clears the local session on your device. Use Delete Account or submit an account deletion request to delete the account itself.
